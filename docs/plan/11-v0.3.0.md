## Findings for part A (2026-11-18, local build, Chrome 1440 px)

- Every looping animation of `box-shadow`, `background`, `background-position`, `left`/`top`, `inset` or
  `clip-path` repaints the whole document layer each frame. On the home, `livepulse` (the live dot) alone made the
  page repaint 119 times a second with nobody touching it (1 per second without it), and the main thread was busy
  about 78 ms of each second. The admin console has the same pattern (`ping`), Services about ten.
- Most running animations were off screen (home 11 of 18, Services 15 of 17).
- The hero ribbon was throttled to 30 fps; slow motion at 30 fps reads as stutter on 60 Hz and faster screens.
